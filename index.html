The product

Hardware that helps renewable energy systems waste less.

We are building a product for renewable energy systems. Its purpose is efficiency: more of the energy a system generates becomes energy that can be used. We will share the technical detail when the product is ready to show.

About Sahastitwa

A product company, building in India.

Sahastitwa Solutions Private Limited is developing a product that improves the efficiency of renewable energy systems.

Alongside that product, we provide custom hardware for indigenous development, design, and manufacturing in India.

The name means coexistence: a product and a custom-hardware practice, held together without one being mistaken for the other.

Questions buyers ask

Straight answers before a first conversation.

What does Sahastitwa Solutions do?

We are a product company. The product is hardware that improves the efficiency of renewable energy systems. We also provide custom hardware for indigenous development, design, and manufacturing in India.

What does the product do?

It is being developed so renewable energy systems can use more of the energy they generate. We are not publishing the internal design yet. Ask us directly if you want to know where the work stands.

Do you also build custom hardware?

Yes. That work supports development, design, and manufacturing in India. It sits beside the product. The custom hardware page describes how that engagement runs.
