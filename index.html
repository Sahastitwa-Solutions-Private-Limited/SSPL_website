How the work is shaped

From an energy or machine problem to a prototype you can test.

A useful system has to harvest or receive energy, hold it, control it, and put it to work under real constraints.

01

Harvest and convert

Make renewable and otherwise unused energy available as controlled electrical power.

02

Store and protect

Hold energy so supply is not limited to the moment it is generated, with protection suited to the duty.

03

Control in the field

Embedded control matches available power and machine behaviour to changing load.

04

Automate the task

Robotics and industrial automation turn that control into repeatable work on the line or in the plant.

Questions buyers ask

Straight answers before a first conversation.

What does Sahastitwa Solutions do?

We design and prototype indigenous hardware for renewable energy, power electronics, embedded systems, robotics, and industrial automation. The work covers research, a working prototype, and development of the system your duty requires.

Who do you work with?

Industrial plants, energy-intensive businesses, renewable-energy companies, OEMs, and technology teams that need custom engineering rather than an off-the-shelf unit.

Do you sell a catalogue product?

No. We start from the operating problem, constraints, and definition of success, then research and prototype the hardware and control approach that fits.
