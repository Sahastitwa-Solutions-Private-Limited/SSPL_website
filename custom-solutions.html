What our name means

Disciplines that stay distinct and still work as one system.

Sahastitwa means coexistence: people and specialisms contributing without one of them erasing the others.

In practice that means we do not arrive with a fixed product. We listen to the duty, share the relevant expertise, and build with the people who know the plant, the machine, or the energy system.

How an engagement runs

Your context first. The prototype second.

Custom engineering starts when the problem, the constraints, and the definition of success are shared.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Study the problem, operating environment, users, and practical constraints.

  2. 02

    Co-create

    Explore feasible directions with your team and the people who know the duty.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    Build the smallest meaningful proof that reduces the technical uncertainty.

  4. 04

    Learn and refine

    Test, document, and improve the solution from evidence in real use.

Before you write

What this engagement is, and what it is not.

Which engineering disciplines do you cover?

Power electronics, embedded systems, robotics, and industrial automation. A project can stay in one of those, or combine hardware, firmware, software, and connected monitoring.

What does a typical engagement look like?

We study the problem and constraints, explore feasible directions with your team, build the smallest prototype that answers the open technical question, then refine it from real use.

Can project details stay confidential?

Yes. This site does not stand in for a case study. Relevant experience is discussed directly, and client operating data stays with the people who need it.

Where is Sahastitwa Solutions based?

Sahastitwa Solutions Private Limited is an Indian engineering company. We work with industrial, energy, OEM, and technology teams that need hardware developed for their duty.
